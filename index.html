Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cloasta? +

Cloasta is an AI workflow optimizer and memory extension that allows you to capture, refine, and continue your conversations across multiple AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

How does AI session sync work? +

Our extension silently indexes your queries locally and allows you to instantly recall and transfer conversation context between different AI tabs via semantic search.

Can I transfer conversations between ChatGPT and Claude? +

Yes, Cloasta provides cross-model sync, letting you seamlessly mirror and migrate your chat history and context from ChatGPT directly to Claude or Gemini.

Does Cloasta support Gemini? +

Yes, Cloasta is fully cloud agnostic and supports major AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and local Ollama instances.

Is Cloasta a Chrome extension? +

Cloasta is a lightweight Chrome extension built with native JavaScript — zero bloat, zero performance impact on your browser. Simply download the zip file, go to chrome://extensions, enable Developer Mode, and drag and drop to install. Takes under 2 minutes.

How does AI Passport work? +

AI Passport lets you save complex system instructions, personas, and prompts once, and use them universally across any supported AI platform.
